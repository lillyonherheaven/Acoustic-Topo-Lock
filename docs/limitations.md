# Limitations

- Recordings from the originally planned 20 participants and four project rooms are unavailable.
- ECAPA comes from a pretrained VoxCeleb model and was never fitted on project participants.
- ASVspoof 2019 PA supports playback-countermeasure research. The challenge corpus uses simulated replay conditions and cannot validate the intended four rooms.
- Room compatibility depends on room-labeled training data. A matching room can contain replayed speech, and speech-envelope decay features are proxies.
- Full verification requires all three checks. Real-mode outcomes are research decisions gated by provisional or development thresholds.
- Uploaded files cannot establish capture provenance. Deepfakes, new devices, and new rooms may lead to failures. The project makes no claims of resistance to all spoofs or acoustic reproduction.
- Models and measurements can shift with device, room, gain, speech content, and background noise. Generalization to unseen devices needs purpose-built evaluation.
